Why Uncover?

What if the first thing someone liked about you was your conversation?

We're building a slower, more intentional way to meet people—one that gives personality a chance to arrive before appearance takes over.

Our principle

A connection should be mutual at every step. Talk privately. Decide if you like the person. Ask to reveal. Only unlock the profile when both people agree.
