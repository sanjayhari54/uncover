Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Uncover is built around privacy: you talk first and reveal your identity only when you both choose to. This policy explains how [your full name or business name] (“we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use Uncover. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. What we collect

InformationWhen it is collected
Email address and passwordWhen you create an account. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored in hashed form; we cannot read it.
Display name, date of birth, age, gender, general location, bio, interests, what you are looking for, and a profile photo if you add oneWhen you create or edit your profile.
MessagesWhen you chat with another member.
Likes and approvals, blocks, and reportsWhen you approve a connection, block someone, or report someone.
Usage information: when you were last active, how many free conversations you have used, notifications, and your plan statusAs you use the Service.
Technical information such as your IP address and device or browser typeAutomatically, when your browser connects to our hosting and database services.

We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, or precise location. Please do not put private contact details in your bio or messages.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, run private conversations, and show you other members in Discover.
  • To reveal your profile to another member only after both of you approve the connection.
  • To enforce the free conversation limit and manage your plan.
  • To keep members safe: handling blocks and reports, preventing abuse, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To send service messages such as email confirmation and security notices.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What other members can see

  • In Discover and before a reveal: other members see an anonymous card with your age, the general location you entered, your bio, your interests, and what you are looking for. They do not see your name, photo, or email. Because a bio or location can sometimes identify a person, please keep them general.
  • After both of you approve: the other person can see your display name, age, gender, location, bio, interests, what you are looking for, and your photo if you added one.
  • Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other members.
  • You can turn off “Show me in Discover” on your profile page at any time.

4. Who handles your data for us

We use trusted providers to run Uncover. They process data only to provide their services to us.

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and real-time messaging. Our project is hosted in Singapore, so your data is processed outside India.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.
  • jsDelivr: delivers a code library our pages need to work.
  • Google Fonts: delivers the fonts used on the site. Loading fonts shares your IP address with Google.

When paid plans launch, a payment provider will process your payment details. We will not see or store your full card details, and we will update this policy before we begin collecting payments.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of members.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to hold temporary form data. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile information is kept while your account is active.
  • Messages are kept while the conversation exists so that you and the other person can read it.
  • Safety reports and block records may be kept after an account is deleted for as long as needed to protect members and meet legal obligations.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except what we must keep as described above.

7. Security

We use access controls in our database so that members can only read their own data and the information they are entitled to see. Data is encrypted in transit. Messages are stored on our servers and are not end-to-end encrypted, which means we can access them if needed to investigate a report or meet a legal duty. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or complete it (you can also edit most details yourself on your profile page);
  • ask us to delete your account and personal data;
  • withdraw your consent, which may mean we can no longer provide the Service;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you are unable to; and
  • raise a complaint about how your data is handled.

To use these rights, email us at [your contact email]. If we have not resolved your complaint, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

9. Age limit

Uncover is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using Uncover, please report the account or email us, and we will act on it quickly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Uncover grows. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

11. Contact and grievances

Grievance contact: [name of the person responsible], [your contact email]. We aim to respond within [7] days.
